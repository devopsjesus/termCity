KILLING BLOW: ARROW

The first two swings use the six approved poses. The right hand keeps moving through the low gather while the left catches the handle, then slides down to sit just above the right hand. Both elbows open outward during the overhead lift; the right wrist follows its forearm as the grip rolls around the handle. Both hands bring the upright hammer directly above the head, slowing into a slightly backward-leaning load with the striking face forward. Only then does the torso initiate the downswing, with the arms following. Slow two-handed recovery completes the loop. Key-pose review retains the video comparisons. This is authored pose adaptation, not motion capture or a physics simulation.

All orcs march along the ground arrow. Camera rotation does not redirect their travel or attacks; torso rotation is part of the spear delivery.

Arrow: drop weapon, clutch with both hands, then release into a loose collapse. Blade: stagger and side fall. Crushing: knees give way and the torso folds. Gravity-led jointed arms settle with the body; this is procedural motion, not a physics simulation. No gore.

Armor stays attached; weapons are released during death. Reduced-motion preference starts paused; hidden tabs suspend playback.
